    <p class="section__eyebrow">Research</p>
    <h1 class="page-title">Microbial ecology across hosts and habitats</h1>
    <p class="page-lede">
      My research asks how microbial communities assemble, persist, and influence the health of the
      hosts and ecosystems they inhabit — using tools from microbial ecology, genomics, and
      biogeochemistry to connect community structure to function.
    </p>

    <div class="topics">
      <div class="topic-card">
        <span class="topic-card__index">01</span>
        <h3>Host-microbe interactions &amp; disease ecology</h3>
        <p>
          Working with the Woodhams Lab as part of the RIBBiTR project, I study how skin-associated
          bacteria and fungi protect amphibians against the chytrid fungus <em>Batrachochytrium
          dendrobatidis</em> and other pathogens, and how habitat connectivity and immune complexity
          shape those defenses across species and climates.
        </p>
      </div>

      <div class="topic-card">
        <span class="topic-card__index">02</span>
        <h3>Fungal disease ecology &amp; mycology</h3>
        <p>
          I investigate the amphibian and bat mycobiome — the often-overlooked fungal half of the
          microbiome — and its role in antifungal defense, including how bat mycobiomes are structured
          by geography, species, and behavior in the American Southwest.
        </p>
      </div>

      <div class="topic-card">
        <span class="topic-card__index">03</span>
        <h3>Salt marsh biogeochemistry</h3>
        <p>
          My doctoral and postdoctoral research examined how nutrient loading and diel cycles drive
          microbial dormancy, denitrification, and nitrous oxide flux in salt marsh sediments,
          largely through long-term fertilization experiments at the Plum Island Estuary (the TIDE project).
        </p>
      </div>

      <div class="topic-card">
        <span class="topic-card__index">04</span>
        <h3>Microbial dormancy &amp; community dynamics</h3>
        <p>
          During my postdoc in the Shade Lab, I studied how dormancy shapes trait-based patterns of
          microbial activity and resilience — from resource pulses in soil to secondary succession —
          and how accounting for dormant taxa changes our picture of community stability.
        </p>
      </div>

      <div class="topic-card">
        <span class="topic-card__index">05</span>
        <h3>Environmental &amp; agricultural microbiomes</h3>
        <p>
          I've contributed to work on plant-, wastewater-, and insect-associated microbiomes,
          including rhizosphere communities of invasive and crop plants, nitrogen-removing wastewater
          treatment systems, and mosquito microbiomes in Rwanda.
        </p>
      </div>

      <div class="topic-card">
        <span class="topic-card__index">06</span>
        <h3>Science education &amp; broadening participation</h3>
        <p>
          Alongside research, I design research-immersion courses and workshops — including the
          IMSD Research Immersion Lab at UMass Boston — that give underrepresented students in
          STEM hands-on experience with molecular and bioinformatic techniques.
        </p>
      </div>
    </div>
